**Ember Court** is an original fantasy card-duel game: you face the Warden's court across a felt table with a deck built from four suits — Ember (burn), Tide (heal & draw), Thorn (block & weaken) and Gale (tempo & ember) — each with its own combo when you play two of a suit in a turn. Read the rival's declared intent, counter it with shield or burst, draft new cards and pick relics between acts, and fight through a 3-act campaign of rivals and bosses, or chase score in Endless Duel.

## Run it

```
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — click a card (or press 1–7) to play it, Enter ends your turn, Esc pauses. No build step, no network: everything the game needs is in `game/`.
